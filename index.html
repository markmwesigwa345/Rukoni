Who We Are

A Legacy of Excellence Since 1941

From the hills of Kakorogoto in Ntungamo District, Rukoni Secondary School has shaped leaders for over 80 years — blending rigorous academics with moral formation, guided by our motto: “Work and Never Tire.”

Our Identity

Mission, Values & Prophecy

Our Mission

To produce studious, marketable, community-oriented and God-fearing citizens through provision of formal education.

Our Vision

To transform into persons of competence, conscience, compassion, character and faith in collaboration with all to build up a humane society.

Faith

Grounded in God, guided by conscience

Excellence

Academic rigour, intellectual competence

Integrity

Honest character, moral discipline

Compassion

Community-oriented, caring citizens

Service

To God, country and humanity

The School Prophecy
“

Rukoni Secondary School shall stand as a mountain of knowledge in the heart of Ruhaama — raising generations of faithful, competent and compassionate leaders who shall illuminate Uganda and the nations beyond with the light of learning, integrity and unwavering faith in God. The seed planted here shall bear fruit in every corner of the earth, and those who pass through these gates shall go forth and never tire in service to God, country and all humanity.

”
