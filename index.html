Who We Are

A Legacy of Excellence in the Heart of Ruhaama East

Rukoni Secondary School stands as a beacon of educational excellence in Ntungamo District. Located in the peaceful community of Kakologoto, Rukoni East, Ruhaama-East Constituency, we have spent decades transforming young minds into responsible, capable, and inspired leaders.

Our institution blends rigorous academic programs with rich extracurricular opportunities, guided by a dedicated team of experienced educators who believe in the unlimited potential of every student. We hold firm to our motto: "Work and Never Tire"
