Who We Are

A Legacy of Excellence Since 1941

From the hills of Kakorogoto in Ntungamo District, Rukoni Secondary School has shaped leaders for over 80 years — blending rigorous academics with moral formation, guided by our motto: “Work and Never Tire.”

Our Identity

Mission, Vision & Prophecy

Our Mission

To produce studious, marketable, community-oriented and God-fearing citizens through provision of formal education.

Our Vision

To transform into persons of competence, conscience, compassion, character and faith in collaboration with all to build up a humane society.

Our Prophecy

To stand as a mountain of knowledge, raising faithful, capable, and compassionate leaders who will tirelessly serve God, country, and humanity globally.
